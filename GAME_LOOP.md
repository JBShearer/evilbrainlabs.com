# SUBLEVEL B — WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS

An on-rails choose-your-own-adventure roguelike with characters, color, and
a branching story. ONE screen. ONE card at a time. NO tabs. NO dashboard.
If Jason's mom and wife can't play it without instructions, it is wrong.

You are a new hire at Evil Brain Labs. You certify you are not human.
You meet people — Supes, Gary, GI Intelligence, the Brain, the Senator,
Benny, Wendy — one scene at a time, written warm and funny. Sometimes a
scene hands you the toybox: three quick taps (what it does / what it is /
who it's for) and you SHIP IT. The newspaper unfolds RIGHT THERE, over the
story — a beat, not a destination. People react in the next scenes. Days
pass on rails. What you shipped comes back into the story later. The story
branches on your choices and your products. You can die; you restart as a
new hire, and the story remembers.

Everything already built is BACKSTAGE for scenes, never a destination:
the pixel art draws the scenes, the cast bible voices them, the news
generator writes the paper that unfolds, the ledger schedules the story's
callbacks, the minigames appear as moments inside scenes. The board is a
character handing you a note. The world is wherever the story takes you.

The build brief, whole and entire: **remake v1's one-card flow with the
labyrinth's art and cast — a curated, branching story where the newspaper
unfolds after every ship. No tabs.**

Everything else this file ever said lives in git history as reference,
not instruction. This page wins. It is the only page.
