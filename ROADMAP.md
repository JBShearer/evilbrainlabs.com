# ROADMAP.md · Evil Brain Labs, after the lights come on
## Phased. Each item earns its place by flow, novelty, or collectability.

## NOW (ships with P1, the registry)
Fifty seed cases and the 3x vocabulary (the launch content package).
Filing rate cap per account per day; sub-24h accounts file as pending.
Terms/report page, privacy page (no trackers, say so proudly).
Public dataset export: nightly JSON dump of the ink layer. A citable
registry is one the press can download.

## NEXT (P2, the game)
Vignette-at-mint (flagged): Sonnet draws a constrained two-color SVG per
card at mint, stored once, deterministic forever. Fallback: geometric.
FOIL mints: 4% of card mints carry a sheen variant. Same triple, same
stats, pure collectability. Foils never affect battles.
THE BINDER: a collection view. Cards by grade, by sin, by class, with
set-completion lines ("all SURVEIL commons from Season 1"). Collection
is retention; the binder is where it lives.
Weekly review as rich HTML email (Resend/SMTP, SPF/DKIM). The Brain's
performance review in a real inbox beats any push notification.
Table party mode UI: winner-stays room with carry pot, spectator seats,
prediction window, the Brain hosting live.
Doom Prior public chart: the circulation-weighted graph, pencil-gray,
updated nightly. This is the talk slide; make it a permalink.

## LATER (P3+)
The Composer (Build 3): K'Dee's sprite library replaces geometry for
canonical entities; player-assembled products as shareable vector cards.
Python pipeline: ingestion, extraction, clustering, gate proposals, cut
sets. Feeds pencil only.
Trading between friends (requires the appeal infrastructure to be
battle-tested first; trades are where disputes live).
Braincaches: QR/NFC drops at real events. Every hackathon becomes a
treasure hunt; every scan mints a signed artifact.
SEASONS proper: quarterly resets, hall of fame, the Brain's earnings
call for a company with no earnings, ascension ballots archived.
Show integration: EBN ticker of freshly shipped products; case-of-the-
week pulled from the registry into episodes; episode QR back to its case.

## SOMEDAY / MAYBE
Guilds as Departments (shared portfolios, department reviews).
Co-op raids vs a Brain mega-product (async, no timers, per doctrine).
Localization (the vocabulary is a controlled list; it translates).
Educational mode: the registry as curriculum, LAUSD-shaped.

## NEVER (restated so the roadmap can't drift)
Cash in, cash out, or brokered trades. A seventh slot. Timed forfeiture.
Individual geolocation. Effects over 1Hz. Negative reviews. A VP of
Greed. Em dashes.
