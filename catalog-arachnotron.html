Arachnotron MK-IV

It climbs the pole so nobody's dad has to.
Kit № 002 · Plans-Built · Class: Octopod, Utility
Plate II — Arachnotron MK-IVDrawn from the plans, which exist. Unlike the robot.

Every year, linemen climb forty feet of splintered cedar in freezing rain because a transformer coughed. The ARACHNOTRON MK-IV is a 14-kilogram carbon-frame octopod that climbs the pole instead — clamps at the crossarm, photographs the fault, torques the hardware, and comes back down when it is finished, all commanded from a lawn chair at a safe and dignified distance.

The MK-I fell off the pole. The MK-II fell off the pole with confidence. The MK-III made it to the top and refused to come down, which the Brain classified as "ambition." The MK-IV is what three honest failures buy you: a machine whose grip is a decision, whose radio does not panic, and whose whole terrible job is to make the most dangerous ordinary work in your town boring.

What arrives is not a robot. What arrives is better: the complete architecture and build plan for the prototype — every subsystem, every trade we made and why, the reference parts philosophy, and the three failure modes we already found so you can discover exciting new ones. Coyote-style. You build it. That was always the point.
