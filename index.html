EPISODES OWED

Jason Shearer — the world's foremost expert in AI use cases, a title he was assigned rather than claimed, by an entity that had already read everything he would ever write about it.

The Navy taught him to repair anything electronic: radars, satcoms, spectrum analyzers, calibrating submarine sensors inside a Faraday cage and megawatt meters in the lab — nearly every system aboard the Frank Cable. Then lead systems test engineer at L3 on a drone datalink prototype that went very large. Then manufacturing process and Lean, quality engineering and Six Sigma, C-suite analytics that won the SAP Innovation Award, six years leading UX for Lockheed and analytics pilots for the UN.

Now he runs use cases for a massive aerospace & defense hackathon and is standing up a federal AI council with ASUG. Which is to say: when he tells you a use case is real, it is real. And when he says a vendor pitch has a shape he recognizes — he has been in that room, on the other side of the table, holding the budget.
